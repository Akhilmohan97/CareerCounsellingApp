## ?? Glass Morphism Effect

The "glassy" look uses:
1. **Light backgrounds** (#F7FAFC, #FFFFFF)
2. **Subtle borders** (1px #E2E8F0)
3. **Rounded corners** (12-16px)
4. **Transparency/opacity** (80-90%)
5. **Smooth transitions** (0.2s)
6. **Professional shadows** (subtle, not heavy)
