## ?? How to Use

### 3 Simple Steps

1. **Generate AI Interpretation** (if not already done)
   - Click "?? Generate AI Counsellor Notes" button

2. **Find Copy Button**
   - Look for green "?? Copy Prompt" button
   - Located at top-right of AI section

3. **Copy & Use**
   - Click the button
   - Button shows "? Copied!" for 2 seconds
   - Prompt is now in your clipboard
   - Paste into ChatGPT (Ctrl+V)
